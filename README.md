## ⚡ What is AbAI?

**AbAI** (*Assisted by AI*) and the **AbAI-ed** status designation provide an open, transparent framework for co-creation between human creators and machine intelligence. 

Rather than viewing artificial intelligence as an autonomous substitute for human ingenuity, **AbAI** defines AI strictly as an acceleration engine, creative sparring partner, and compilation layer operated under human executive direction.

---

## 📐 The 3 Pillars

To carry the **AbAI-ed** designation, a project or release must fulfill:

1. **Human Architecture:** Creative vision, structural logic, and constraints originate from human intent.
2. **Algorithmic Co-Production:** AI tools are actively leveraged for syntax generation, synthesis, iteration, or refinement.
3. **Executive Verification & Taste:** Every output is tested, curated, and debugged by the human director. No unvetted raw dumps.
